# Guardian Terms of Use (draft)

**This is a draft for hackathon/demo use.**

## What Guardian does and doesn't do

Guardian helps you monitor and respond to household events. It is a
decision-support and automation tool, **not a security system, medical
device, or emergency service**. It does not replace smoke detectors,
alarm systems, medical alert devices, or calling emergency services
directly when a real emergency occurs.

## Autonomy limits (by design, not just policy)

Guardian operates on a three-tier model:

- **Inform**: Guardian may act and tell you afterward.
- **Ask**: Guardian proposes an action and waits for your confirmation.
- **Escalate**: urgent situations that need your immediate attention.

Some actions (e.g. unlocking a door) are hard constraints that Guardian
will never perform autonomously regardless of confidence, as
implemented in the decision engine. You are responsible for
configuring policies appropriately for your household and for
responding promptly to "ask" and "escalate" notifications — Guardian
does not act as a substitute for human judgment or a monitored
security service.

## Your responsibilities

- Keep household member and routine information accurate — Guardian's
  reasoning depends on it.
- Do not rely on Guardian as your sole safety mechanism for vulnerable
  household members.
- You are responsible for the accounts, devices (Ring, etc.), and AWS
  infrastructure you connect to Guardian.
