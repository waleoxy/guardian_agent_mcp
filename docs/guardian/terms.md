# Guardian Terms of Use

**Last updated: September 2025. These terms cover the Guardian household safety agent as submitted for the Alexa+ MCP Hackathon.**

## What Guardian does and doesn't do

Guardian helps you monitor and respond to household events. It is a decision-support and automation tool, **not a security system, medical device, or emergency service**. It does not replace smoke detectors, alarm systems, medical alert devices, or calling emergency services directly when a real emergency occurs.

## Autonomy limits (by design, not just policy)

Guardian operates on a three-tier model:

- **Inform**: Guardian may act and tell you afterward.
- **Ask**: Guardian proposes an action and waits for your confirmation.
- **Escalate**: urgent situations that need your immediate attention.

Some actions (e.g. unlocking a door) are hard constraints that Guardian will never perform autonomously regardless of confidence. You are responsible for configuring policies appropriately for your household and for responding promptly to "ask" and "escalate" notifications.

## Your responsibilities

- Keep household member and routine information accurate — Guardian's reasoning depends on it.
- Do not rely on Guardian as your sole safety mechanism for vulnerable household members.
- You are responsible for the accounts, devices (Ring, etc.), and AWS infrastructure you connect to Guardian.

## Service availability

Guardian is provided as-is for hackathon and demonstration purposes, with no uptime guarantee. It depends on third-party services (Amazon Alexa+, AWS, Ring) being available.

## Changes

Material changes will be reflected in an updated "Last updated" date above and a commit to the repository.
